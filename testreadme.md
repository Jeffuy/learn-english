# Book quiz catalogue

The app contains eight quizzes based on the eight units of *B2 First Formula Course Book*. Each quiz has 50 original multiple-choice questions. The examples are not copied from the book, and every question stays within the language and themes introduced in its unit.

## Unit 1: Blue

- Phrasal verbs
- Phrases with prepositions
- Present simple and present continuous
- State verbs
- Present perfect simple and continuous
- Unusual natural phenomena
- Art, colour and La Casa Azul
- Routines, media and travel preferences

## Unit 2: Orange

- Verb + noun collocations
- Negative prefixes
- Past simple, past continuous and past perfect
- `used to`
- Film and visual storytelling
- Fake tanning
- Childhood beliefs
- Pumpkin spice and gifts

## Unit 3: White

- Phrases with `make` and `do`
- Photography phrasal verbs
- Future forms
- Salt and food
- Honesty and white lies
- Black-and-white photography
- Thrill sports
- Parties, plans and gifts

## Unit 4: Pink

- Food phrasal verbs
- Noun suffixes
- Reported statements, questions and commands
- Candyfloss
- Embarrassment
- Pink cities
- Colour and gender
- Optimism and festival reviews

## Unit 5: Black

- Adjective + noun collocations
- Verb patterns
- Zero, first, second and third conditionals
- Other conditional structures
- Vantablack
- Fashion and shopping
- Coffee culture
- Black Friday
- Air pollution and night-time visibility

## Unit 6: Red

- Compound words
- Adjective suffixes
- Comparative and superlative structures
- Chilli peppers
- Red-letter days
- Red-carpet events
- Anger and seeing red
- Fundraising

## Unit 7: Green

- Phrasal verbs
- Easily confused words
- Defining and non-defining relative clauses
- Reduced relative clauses
- Dangers in Victorian life
- Green technology and sustainable products
- Business and money
- Green spaces
- Story dilemmas

## Unit 8: Yellow

- Adverb + adjective collocations
- Adjective suffixes
- Passive forms
- Passive reporting structures
- Honey bees
- Seasons
- Emojis and communication
- Food sculpture
- Friendship
- Clothes, culture and outdoor life

## Totals

- 8 quizzes
- 50 questions per quiz
- 400 questions in total
- 4 answer options per question
- No repeated question text
